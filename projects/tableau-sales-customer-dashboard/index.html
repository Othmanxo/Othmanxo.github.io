Dashboard Views

The workbook includes dedicated views for sales performance and customer performance.

Analysis Coverage

  • Sales, profit, and quantity KPI tracking with current-year versus previous-year comparison.
  • Subcategory-level sales and profit analysis to identify stronger and weaker product areas.
  • Sales and profit trend views highlighting above-average and below-average periods.
  • Customer distribution by number of orders and top customers ranked by profit.

Workbook Details

  • Built in Tableau with two dashboard tabs: Sales Dashboard and Customer Dashboard.
  • Packaged workbook includes a Hyper extract and source tables for orders, customers, products, and location.
  • Uses a year selection parameter set to 2023 for current-year KPI comparisons.
  • Includes dashboard navigation and filter controls for category, subcategory, region, state, and city.
