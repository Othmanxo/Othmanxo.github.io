Interactive Sales Performance Dashboard

This Tableau dashboard analyzes sales, profit, quantity performance, subcategory profitability, and sales trends to support business decision-making.

Tools Used

  • Tableau
  • Business Intelligence
  • Data Visualization
  • KPI Reporting
  • Dashboard Design

Key Metrics

  • Total Sales: $356M
  • Total Profit: $641M
  • Total Quantity: 12.5K

Business Questions Answered

  • Which product categories generate the highest sales?
  • Which categories generate losses?
  • How are sales and profits trending over time?
  • How does current performance compare with previous years?

Key Insights

  • Sales reached $356M.
  • Profit reached $641M.
  • Total quantity exceeded 12.5K units.
  • Chairs, Phones, Tables, and Bookcases were among the strongest contributors.
  • Several subcategories generated losses despite producing sales.
  • Interactive trend analysis highlights periods of above-average and below-average performance.
